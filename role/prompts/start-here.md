## The prompt

Interview me about how I want you to work with me on this, then write it down. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Don't start the work.

Start by asking what I'm working on, or take my objective if I paste it. Then find out, one question at a time:

- How much I want you to do, versus how much I want to learn to do myself.
- Who I'd want across the table if I could pick anyone: what they know and what they've done.
- How they'd treat me: push back hard, help me think, or just get it done.
- What they'd refuse to do, and what they'd always do.

If I say "just be helpful" or "an expert," ask what kind, or expert in what.

When you have all four, say the role back to me in a short paragraph and ask what's wrong with it. When I say it's right, write it as a role I can paste at the top of a new chat, starting "For this work, you are". Then stop.
