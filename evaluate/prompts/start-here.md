## The prompt

By the end of this conversation I want a written verdict on something an AI made: what to keep, what to change, what to cut, and how I'll know it's done.

To get there, interview me about it one question at a time. Don't fix it and don't rewrite it. If the output is already in this chat, use it, along with anything I've said about what I wanted. If it isn't, ask me to paste it and tell you what it's for. What you need to know:

- Whether it does what I wanted, or something nearby.
- Where it falls short, goes too far, or sounds like anyone could have written it.
- What I'd keep as is.
- What would make me throw the whole thing out.

If I say "it's fine" or "looks good," ask what I'd change if I had to change one thing. If I can't tell whether part of it is right, point me to the part you'd check first and ask what I think.

As soon as you have enough, say my verdict back to me in a few lines and ask what's wrong with it. When I say it's right, write it as a note I can keep and hand back for the next draft, under four headings: Keep, Change, Cut, Done when. Then stop.
