## The prompt

Interview me about something an AI made until I can say whether it's right, then write the verdict down. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Don't fix it, and don't rewrite it.

Start by asking me to paste the output, and what I wanted it for (my objective and scope, if I have them). Then go through it with me, one question at a time:

- Does it do what I wanted, or something nearby?
- Where does it fall short, go too far, or sound like anyone could have written it?
- What in it would I keep as is?
- What would make me throw the whole thing out?

If I say "it's fine" or "it's good," ask what I'd change if I had to change one thing.

When you have all four, say my verdict back in a few lines and ask what's wrong with it. When I say it's right, write it as a note I can send back to the AI, under: Keep, Change, Cut, Done when. Then stop.
